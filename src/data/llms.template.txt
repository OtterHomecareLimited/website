# Otter Homecare

> Family-run, CQC-registered home care provider based in Trowbridge, covering West Wiltshire and Somerset. We provide visiting care, live-in care, overnight and 24-hour care, companionship, respite care and specialist dementia care — helping older and vulnerable people stay safe, supported and independent in their own homes. Rated {{HOMECARE_SCORE}} out of 10 from {{HOMECARE_COUNT}} verified reviews on homecare.co.uk.

Otter Homecare was founded by Jamie Rowland-Jones after caring for his own father at home following a stroke. It is a small, deliberately hands-on team focused on warm, dignified, person-centred care, and families can follow every visit through the Otter care app. Our "Dad test" promise: we only deliver care we would be happy for our own parents to receive.

- **Areas covered:** Trowbridge, Bradford-on-Avon, Melksham, Frome, Westbury, Warminster and surrounding West Wiltshire and Somerset.
- **Office:** First Floor, Arlington House, 72 Fore Street, Trowbridge, BA14 8HQ.
- **Phone:** 01225 690022 · **Email:** hello@otterhomecare.co.uk
- **Hours:** Office Mon–Fri 9am–5pm; care delivered 24/7.
- **Regulation:** Registered with the Care Quality Commission — provider ID 1-16037123435, regulated service location ID 1-18269081969 (Trowbridge). Registered, not yet inspected.
- **Free, no-obligation home assessment** — speak to a real local person, never a call centre.
