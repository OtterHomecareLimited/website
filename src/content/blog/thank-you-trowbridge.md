### **Otter Homecare at the Weavers Market – Saturday 11th October 2025**

Last weekend, on Saturday 11th October, Otter Homecare joined the bustling Trowbridge Weavers Market on Fore Street for a day of community connection, conversation, and care.

It was a fantastic day meeting so many friendly faces — from local residents curious about our services, to those exploring a new career in care, to visitors who simply stopped by for a chat. We truly appreciate everyone who took the time to visit our stand and learn more about what makes Otter Homecare special.

Our Care Coordinator, Maria, did a brilliant job representing the team, sharing insights into our values of *kindness, connection, and quality care.* The stand featured our familiar blue Otter Homecare banners, some autumnal touches, and of course our much-loved otter mascots.

We loved hearing stories from local families and care professionals who share our belief that care should always be delivered with warmth and respect. Events like the Weavers Market remind us how important community is — and how many people care deeply about making a positive difference in the lives of others.

Thank you again to everyone who stopped by. We’re already looking forward to taking part in more community events across Wiltshire and Somerset in the months ahead.
