We are excited to announce the launch of our Otter Homecare Facebook page, a new online channel for updating our community.

Otter Homecare arose from the personal experiences of our founder, who, after becoming the primary caregiver for their father following a stroke, realised the urgent need for kinder, more empathetic homecare. Influenced by two decades of living in Asia, a place renowned for its respect and kindness towards the elderly, our founder developed a distinct approach to homecare that is as unique as it is effective.

Our team of professional caregivers are experts in a variety of services, including quality Live-in care, Personal care, Dementia care, and Companionship care. We're passionate about what we do, and that passion shines through in our day-to-day activities, creating a positive environment for all our clients.

Our Facebook page is an extension of our mission to create a supportive, informed community. We'll use this platform to share updates about our activities and offer practical advice around homecare in Wiltshire and Somerset. We believe that knowledge is empowering, and we want to equip you with the insights and tools necessary to navigate the homecare journey with confidence and peace of mind.

So whether you're a current client, considering our services, or simply interested in learning more about homecare, we welcome you to follow our Facebook page to connect with us and become a part of our community.

We thank you for considering Otter Homecare to be a part of your family's journey towards kinder homecare.

Visit our Facebook page at [www.facebook.com/otterhomecare](http://www.facebook.com/otterhomecare) to stay connected and updated. We hope to see you there soon!
