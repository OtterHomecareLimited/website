Navigating the financial landscape of homecare can be daunting, but it is crucial to understand that you have several options at your disposal. At Otter Homecare, we're dedicated to guiding you through the process, ensuring you have the information you need to make informed decisions. Let's explore some of the most common funding options for homecare services in the UK.

**NHS Continuing Healthcare**

For individuals with long-term health issues, the National Health Service (NHS) provides a package known as NHS Continuing Healthcare. This package fully funds homecare or care home services for those with a "primary health need". The funding is not means-tested, and it covers 100% of care costs, including personal care and healthcare needs.

**Local Authority Funding**

Should you not qualify for NHS Continuing Healthcare, your local authority can provide funding assistance. This requires an assessment of your care needs followed by a financial means test. The amount of funding you receive depends on your income, savings, and the level of care you require.

**Self-Funded Homecare**

Some individuals choose to self-fund their homecare. This is often the case if they do not qualify for government support or if they prefer more autonomy over their care choices. Here at Otter Homecare, we offer various care packages and will work with you to create a custom plan that suits your budget.

**Attendance Allowance**

Attendance Allowance is a tax-free benefit for those aged 65 and over who need assistance due to a physical or mental disability. It's worth noting that this benefit is not means-tested, meaning it's available regardless of income or savings.

**Direct Payments**

If you're eligible for local authority funding, you can opt to receive direct payments. This gives you the freedom to pay for your care services directly, offering you more choice and control over your care.

**Insurance Policies**

Certain insurance policies, like long-term care insurance or immediate needs annuities, can cover homecare costs. It's crucial to talk with a financial advisor or insurance expert to understand these options fully.

By providing a comprehensive overview of your funding options, we hope to alleviate some of your concerns. Always remember to seek advice from a financial advisor or your local authority to understand all the options available to you. Financing homecare shouldn't be a barrier to receiving the care you or your loved ones need.
