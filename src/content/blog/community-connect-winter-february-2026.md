Hello everyone,

As we settle into 2026, we wanted to share some of what's been happening at Otter Homecare —
including the results of our recent client survey and, just as importantly, what we're actually
doing in response.

## What you told us

Every winter we ask the families we support what's working and what isn't. The headline this year
was lovely to read: the overwhelming majority of you told us the thing that matters most is seeing
the **same familiar faces** — and that our Care Professionals take the time to do things properly,
not just tick a list.

You also gave us some clear, fair challenges, and we'd rather act on those than bury them.

## What we're changing in response

- **More consistency in your rota.** We already track carer continuity every single week, and we're
  using that data to tighten things further so you see familiar faces even more reliably.
- **Clearer communication.** A few of you asked for more proactive updates between visits — so we're
  making fuller use of the care app, where you can already see every visit, note and medication in
  real time.
- **More community moments.** The Otter Café has been such a joy that we're committing to keep it
  free and monthly through 2026.

## Coming up at the Otter Café

Our next gatherings are at **Staverton Village Hall** — free, warm, and open to everyone, whether
you're a client, a family member, or just fancy a cup of tea and good company. Keep an eye out for
dates, and do come along.

Thank you, as always, for trusting us with the people you love.

*— Jamie & the Otter Homecare team*
