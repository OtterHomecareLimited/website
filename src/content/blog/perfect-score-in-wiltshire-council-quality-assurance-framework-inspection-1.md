We’re thrilled to share that Otter Homecare has achieved a **0 Risk Rating** in our recent Quality Assurance Framework (QAF) inspection conducted by Wiltshire Council – the best possible score!

The inspection evaluated Otter Homecare across essential areas that reflect the quality of our care services:

- **Person-Centred Care** – Ensuring our clients receive personalised, compassionate care tailored to their unique needs.

- **Staff Knowledge and Recruitment** – Demonstrating our commitment to hiring skilled Care Professionals and maintaining high standards through ongoing training.

- **Leadership and Safety** – Upholding a safe and supportive environment for both our clients and staff through effective leadership and quality assurance practices.

This perfect score is a testament to the hard work and dedication of our Care Professionals, who go above and beyond to support our clients in Wiltshire and Somerset. While Otter Homecare is still in its early days, this recognition from Wiltshire Council is really encouraging as we continue to grow and make a positive impact in the community.

A heartfelt thank you to our clients, families, and the entire Otter Homecare team – together, we’re building something truly special! 💙
