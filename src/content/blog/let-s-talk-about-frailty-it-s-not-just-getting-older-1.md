When we talk about getting older, one word that comes up a lot is "frailty." You might have heard it before, but what does it actually mean? It's something many of us and our loved ones might face, but it's not always talked about. So, let's break it down and see how Otter Homecare might be able to help.

**What Is Frailty?**

Imagine your body is like a car. Over time, it might not run as smoothly as it used to. Frailty is when your body starts having a bit more trouble doing everyday things. It's not just about being older; it's about your body feeling weaker and needing a bit more care to keep going strong.

**How Can You Tell?**

There are a few signs that someone might be getting frail:

- Losing weight without trying

- Feeling really tired a lot

- Not being as strong as before

- Moving more slowly

- Not being as active

If this sounds like you or someone you care about, it's a good idea to talk to a doctor or a health professional. They can help figure out the best way to support you.
